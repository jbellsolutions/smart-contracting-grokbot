# dream-100-draft

Role: dream100. Every step runs inside the gates in `repo/GATES.md` and the guards in `repo/guards/SKILL.md`.

1. Read `repo/kit/voice.md` (Jesse's own rules) and the partner type's section of `repo/kit/partner-types.md`.
2. The first email, under 120 words: their name; one true line about them from their own site; the quake-drain story in one sentence; what Smart Contracting does for their customers (a CCTV inspection with a written report every time); the ask: "worth a ten-minute chat?". Sign off as Jesse with the partners address and the office number.
3. Never: the fee, a price, a made-up job or testimonial, "AI", or links beyond the website.
4. Follow-ups: day 4 is two lines, a new angle; day 10 is one line, permission to close. Then stop.
5. Write the item (ITEM, ROLE, WHY with the source link, the PAYLOAD with TO, SUBJECT and TEXT, then HASH via `approval.py hash`).
